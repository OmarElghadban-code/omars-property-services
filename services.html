BEFORE YOU BOOK

Our booking & payment policy

Clear expectations and no surprise charges.

Minimum payment at booking

To reserve a cleaning appointment, customers pay the minimum service charge upfront. Standard residential cleaning starts at $120 for up to three bedrooms (3 hours) or $200 for four or more bedrooms (5 hours). Deep residential cleaning has a $200 minimum (5 hours). Small-office cleaning starts at $135 (3 hours).

Move-out cleaning appointments

Apartment and house move-out cleaning is quoted separately. Request a quote before booking so we can confirm the scope, estimated time, availability, and required payment.

On-site assessment before cleaning

When we arrive, we'll walk through the property, review your cleaning priorities, and assess the time needed. The size, condition, buildup, and requested tasks may mean the job requires more than the minimum hours.

Additional hours require your approval

If extra time is needed, we'll explain the additional hours, the tasks involved, and the cost before starting. Additional residential hours are $40 per hour; additional office hours are $45 per hour. We collect payment for any approved additional hours before cleaning begins. We will never add extra hours or charges without your agreement.

If you prefer to stay within the minimum

We can discuss which tasks to prioritize within your prepaid time. A full cleaning of every requested area may not be possible within the minimum hours. If the scope cannot reasonably be completed within the available time, we'll discuss alternative arrangements before starting.

Cancellation & rescheduling

Cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your appointment for a full refund or free rescheduling. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the appointment are subject to a 50% cancellation fee.

No-shows & property access

No refund is provided for a no-show or if we cannot access the property for the scheduled service. Please arrange access before your appointment.

Refunds

Any applicable refund will be returned to the original payment method.

Please review these terms and the policies presented during booking before confirming your appointment.
