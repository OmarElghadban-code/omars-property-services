BEFORE YOU BOOK

A few helpful answers.

Which service should I choose?

Standard cleaning is a starting point for everyday upkeep. Deep cleaning is for spaces needing more detailed attention. Pre-holiday cleaning focuses on preparing for a gathering, while small-office cleaning is for workplaces. Share your priorities if you are unsure which fits best.

How do I get a quote?

Share your service choice, square footage, bedroom and bathroom counts, and photos of each area to be cleaned. Photos are required before final pricing and job acceptance. Your starting price is an estimate; the scope and final quote are confirmed directly before booking.

Can I request a particular date?

Yes, include your preferred date and any flexibility when you contact us. A requested date is subject to availability; your appointment needs direct confirmation.

What should I mention before a cleaning?

Tell us about pets, access instructions, delicate surfaces, product sensitivities, and areas that need extra attention. Discuss any preparation or supply arrangements before your appointment.
